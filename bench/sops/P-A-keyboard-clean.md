# P-A · Clean a mechanical keyboard

**Procedure ID:** P-A
**Domain:** IT hardware servicing
**Target time:** 25–60 s
**Tools required:** hand, cleaning brush, blower, lint-free cloth, USB cable
**Parts:** keyboard, keycaps, keyboard frame

> Critical steps are marked **(critical)**. The two brushing passes may be performed in
> either order. Every other step is strictly ordered.

1. Tilt the keyboard face-down over the work surface and tap the frame to release loose debris.
2. Brush the upper half of the keycaps with the cleaning brush.
3. Brush the lower half of the keycaps with the cleaning brush.
4. Blow air between the keycaps with the blower.
5. Wipe the keycap surface with a lint-free cloth.
6. Wipe the keyboard frame and edges with the lint-free cloth.
7. Inspect the keycaps and frame for remaining dust or residue.
8. Reconnect the USB cable and confirm the keyboard is recognised. **(critical)**
