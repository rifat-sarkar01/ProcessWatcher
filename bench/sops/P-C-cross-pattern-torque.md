# P-C · 4-bolt cross-pattern torque

**Procedure ID:** P-C
**Domain:** Mechanical assembly
**Target time:** 80–140 s
**Tools required:** torque wrench, socket, torque marker, hand
**Parts:** flange, four M8 bolts, job card

> Critical steps are marked **(critical)**. Steps 7 and 8 may be performed in either order.
> Every other step is strictly ordered.

1. Inspect the flange face and confirm it is clean and free of burrs.
2. Seat the four M8 bolts in the flange holes by hand.
3. Tighten bolt 1 to 25 Nm with the torque wrench and socket. **(critical)**
4. Tighten bolt 3 to 25 Nm with the torque wrench and socket.
5. Tighten bolt 2 to 25 Nm with the torque wrench and socket.
6. Tighten bolt 4 to 25 Nm with the torque wrench and socket.
7. Draw a torque stripe across all four bolts with the torque marker.
8. Record the torque value and inspector initials on the job card. **(critical)**
