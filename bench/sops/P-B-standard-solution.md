# P-B · Prepare 100 ml of a standard sodium chloride solution

**Procedure ID:** P-B
**Domain:** Laboratory chemistry
**Target time:** 100–170 s
**Tools required:** analytical balance, funnel, distilled water bottle, wash bottle, marker, hand
**Parts:** volumetric flask (100 ml), sodium chloride (NaCl), distilled water, label

> Critical steps are marked **(critical)**. Steps 1 and 2 may be performed in either order.
> Every other step is strictly ordered.

1. Clean and dry the 100 ml volumetric flask and set it on the bench.
2. Weigh 0.585 g of sodium chloride on the analytical balance. **(critical)**
3. Transfer the weighed sodium chloride into the volumetric flask through the funnel.
4. Add about 50 ml of distilled water from the distilled water bottle.
5. Swirl the flask until every crystal has dissolved.
6. Top up with distilled water from the wash bottle until the meniscus sits on the 100 ml calibration mark. **(critical)**
7. Stopper the flask and invert it three times to mix.
8. Check the meniscus again at eye level and top up if it has dropped.
9. Label the flask with the substance name, concentration and date using the marker.
