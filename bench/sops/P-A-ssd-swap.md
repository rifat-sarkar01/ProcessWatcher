# P-A · SSD swap in a desktop PC

**Procedure ID:** P-A
**Domain:** IT hardware servicing
**Target time:** 90–150 s
**Tools required:** antistatic wrist strap, Phillips screwdriver, hand
**Parts:** desktop case, side panel, SATA data cable, SATA power cable, old SSD, new SSD, mounting screws, thumbscrews

> Critical steps are marked **(critical)**. Steps 3 and 4 may be performed in either order,
> and steps 7 and 8 may be performed in either order. Every other step is strictly ordered.

1. Power the PC down, unplug the power cable and clip the antistatic wrist strap to bare metal. **(critical)**
2. Remove the two rear thumbscrews and slide the side panel off the desktop case.
3. Unplug the SATA data cable from the old SSD.
4. Unplug the SATA power cable from the old SSD.
5. Remove the two mounting screws with the Phillips screwdriver and slide the old SSD out of the bay.
6. Slide the new SSD into the bay and fasten the two mounting screws with the Phillips screwdriver.
7. Plug the SATA data cable into the new SSD.
8. Plug the SATA power cable into the new SSD.
9. Refit the side panel and tighten the two rear thumbscrews.
10. Reconnect the power cable and boot the machine to confirm the new SSD is detected. **(critical)**
